Reported outcome

The
result.

The supplied one-month report records PKR 47,584.79 in spend and PKR 259,653 in purchase conversion value, resulting in a reported purchase ROAS of 5.46×. The five visible campaign rows show 63 purchases. These figures are advertising-account metrics, not net margin or independently confirmed paid orders.

Interpreting the data

The 63 purchases are from five visible campaign rows, not a confirmed all-account purchase total. Reported purchase ROAS measures attributed revenue divided by ad spend; it does not measure business profit.
