# Meta Ads portfolio source notes

Extracted from the user-supplied ZIP of 11 DOCX reports on October 24, 2026. Document paragraphs, tables and embedded screenshot images were read. The original source documents are unchanged. Published screenshots are lossless WebP encodings of the embedded images.

`lib/meta-cases.mjs` records the source filename, reporting scope, metric origin and reconciliation notes for each case. Dashboard metrics, calculated ratios and reported business outcomes are separate. Messaging conversations are not labelled qualified leads, and narrative revenue is not presented as platform-attributed ROAS.

Two Mughal Homes documents are one case with separate snapshots. Their rows overlap. Anonymous electronics campaigns are not combined, nor are the additional messaging screenshots. Three versions of a small messaging campaign and the two iTeach image variants are not treated as additional clients or additive totals.

Reconciliations:

- Legacy: screenshot spend 200,723.79 PKR and reported revenue 3,000,000 PKR imply 14.95x revenue/spend, not the narrative 30x ROAS.
- Awwal: 2,000,000 reported revenue / 109,773.30 dashboard spend = 18.22x; rounded narrative 20x is not used as platform ROAS.
- Resin: screenshot 311 orders created, 243 purchases, 14,126 conversations and 404,311.67 PKR spend replace the narrative rounded headlines. Reported 4 million revenue is separate (9.89x reported revenue/spend).
- Digikon: screenshot shows 324,435.60 PKR spend, 3,793 new messaging contacts, 4,986 total messaging contacts and 1,848,044 impressions; narrative 352K/3,900+/2M+ is not used as dated dashboard data.
- Iqra: Meta August 1–September 15, 2026 shows 104,182.71 spend and 710,999 purchase value (6.82x calculated value/spend). Shopify August 1–September 10, 2026 shows 719.4K all-channel sales, 75 orders, 2,745 sessions and 1.63% conversion. These periods and attributions are not merged.
- Mughal: earlier snapshot has 69 conversations + 13 Meta leads, not 82 qualified leads. Later snapshot has 19 leads, 71,173.39 spend and 398,532 impressions. Conflicting 40M/45M transaction values and unsupported return claims are omitted from headline metrics.
- AB: 69 leads are from six visible rows; 1,133.04 AED spend is across 15 campaigns. An overall CPL cannot be calculated from that partial numerator. Revenue is narrative-only.
- MBM: 63 purchases are a five-row subtotal. Purchase conversion value 259,653 and spend 47,584.79 are account totals; the screenshot's displayed purchase ROAS is 5.46.
- Electronics C: 500K vs 5,232,900 narrative revenue conflict; revenue omitted. “Per unit cost” in electronics narratives describes assumed order values rather than advertising acquisition costs.
- iTeach: 228 leads and a 1,000 PKR course price establish potential revenue only, not paid enrolments.

No cross-client or cross-currency revenue/spend/ROAS totals are published.
