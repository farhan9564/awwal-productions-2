## Before launching
1. Confirm your production site is https://awwalproductions.com/ and redirects other hostnames to this canonical hostname. If not, update all canonical/structured-data/sitemap URLs.
2. Verify each portfolio project's actual contribution/scope and whether screenshots or metrics can be published.
3. Request explicit client approval for public advertising case study numbers and source links.
4. Connect a real contact backend if you want messages delivered without requiring an email client or WhatsApp.
5. Configure HTTPS, caching, compression, image handling, Google Search Console and analytics with consent appropriate to your markets.
